Whispers of Desire - Part 1

Lila always walked alone by the river. The water shone under the morning sun and birds sang softly. She felt a quiet sadness inside her. Every day seemed the same.

One morning she noticed a small boat tied to the old wooden dock. A boy was sitting inside reading a book. His hair was dark and messy, and his eyes looked kind. Lila felt curious and slowly walked closer.

The boy looked up and smiled. He did not speak. He only nodded. Lila sat on the grass nearby and watched the river. For the first time in many days, she felt calm.

The wind whispered through the trees. Lila felt something change inside her heart. She did not understand it yet.

From that day, she came every morning to see the boy. They did not talk much. They shared silence and the gentle sound of the river. Lila began to feel a warmth she had never felt before.
