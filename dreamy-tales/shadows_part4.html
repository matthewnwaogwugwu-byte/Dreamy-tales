Shadows of the Heart - Part 4

Lina’s heart was pounding. Every corner of the town whispered secrets that felt like warnings. She couldn’t stop thinking about him, the man who had turned her world upside down.

As she entered the old park, memories of laughter and hidden tears flooded her mind. The wind seemed to carry messages from a past she wished she could erase. Every shadow was a reminder of choices she hadn’t made yet.

Suddenly, she heard a faint voice calling her name. Her steps froze. Could it be him? Or was it just her imagination playing tricks on her lonely mind?

Time felt slower than usual. Every second stretched into minutes, and Lina knew that the moment she was waiting for had finally arrived. A revelation that could change everything.
