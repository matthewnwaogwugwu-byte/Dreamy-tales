Shadows of the Heart - Part 1

It was a quiet evening in the small town of Evergreen. Lina walked down the empty street, her thoughts heavy with worry. The shadows of the past seemed to follow her everywhere.

She remembered the day everything changed. The day she met him. He had a kind smile, but there was something hidden behind his eyes. Lina didn’t know that her heart was about to be caught in a storm of emotions she never expected.

The wind whispered through the trees, carrying memories she wished she could forget. Each step she took brought her closer to a decision that could change her life forever.
