Shadows of the Heart - Part 3

The rain poured heavily as Lina ran through the streets of Evergreen. Her heart was pounding, her mind racing with questions she had no answers to. The shadows of betrayal seemed closer than ever.

She remembered the whispered words from the night before — promises that turned out to be lies. Each step she took was heavy with doubt. Could she trust anyone again? Or had her heart been too naive?

Turning a corner, she saw a familiar face, yet unfamiliar in the glow of the street lamps. Memories collided — laughter, tears, and the ache of lost moments. The storm outside mirrored the storm inside her soul.

Lina had to make a choice: confront the past or run from it. She paused at the old bridge, the wind tugging at her hair. Every decision had consequences, and every action would ripple through the lives of those she loved most.
