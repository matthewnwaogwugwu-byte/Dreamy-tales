Shadows of the Heart - Part 6

Lina froze as the figure approached. The moonlight revealed a familiar face—someone she thought she had lost forever. Her heart leapt, but fear tightened her chest.

“Lina,” the voice whispered, soft yet urgent. Every word carried weight, every pause a hidden meaning. She wanted to speak, but the shadows of doubt held her back.

He reached out his hand, and memories flooded back—days of laughter, moments of confusion, and the choices that had led them here. She felt torn between the past and the uncertain future.

The night wind carried secrets she had never known. Every rustle in the trees, every distant sound made her question what was real. She realized that understanding the shadows of the heart meant facing the fears she had buried long ago.
