Shadows of the Heart - Part 2

Lina reached the old bridge at the edge of town. She paused and looked at the flowing river below. The water reflected the last light of the sunset, turning golden with every ripple.

Her mind was still filled with questions. Could she trust him? Could she open her heart again after everything she had gone through? The memories of past mistakes haunted her, but there was also hope — a tiny spark she couldn't ignore.

Suddenly, a soft voice called her name. She turned around and saw him standing there, a gentle smile on his face. He held out a small note, and Lina’s heart raced. The words written would either bring comfort or break her entirely.
